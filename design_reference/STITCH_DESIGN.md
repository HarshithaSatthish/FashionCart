## Brand & Style

This design system embodies high-velocity computational commerce: where editorial fashion meets precision machine learning. The aesthetic combines the disciplined restraint of financial engineering interfaces with the visual polish of modern digital luxury. 

The design narrative is built on three core pillars:
- **Algorithmic Authority:** Visual structures prioritize high signal-to-noise ratio, quantitative telemetry, and instant legibility over decorative excess.
- **Editorial Utility:** Layouts treat product SKUs, association graph paths, and inventory telemetry with the sharpness of an architectural lookbook.
- **Tactile Modernity:** The interface uses deep midnight structural grounding contrasted with an ultra-clean, clinical-grade daylight canvas, accentuated by vibrant mathematical signaling colors.

The dominant visual style is **Contemporary Analytical Minimalist** with structural contrast: an anchoring midnight chrome paired with soft, high-clarity surface elevations, micro-metric tagging, hairline borders, and purposeful typography.

## Colors

The palette balances deep structural permanence with high-visibility analytical accents.

### Role Assignments
- **Primary (`#625BF6`):** Deep indigo/violet. Reserved for primary operational actions, active navigation states, model run triggers, and dominant intelligence indicators.
- **Secondary (`#2563EB`):** Electric precision blue. Used for analytical links, secondary telemetry, graph nodes, and discovery filters.
- **Tertiary (`#10B981`):** Emerald green. Used exclusively for positive growth metrics, validation states, and high confidence/lift thresholds.
- **Neutral (`#111318`):** Deep midnight slate. Powers the persistent navigation frame, critical typography, high-contrast modal surfaces, and data graph hulls.

### Supporting Semantics & Surfaces
- **Canvas Base:** `#F7F8FA` provides an eye-comforting, crisp alternative to pure white.
- **Surface Elevation (Cards, Panels):** `#FFFFFF` pure white surfaces sit atop the canvas base.
- **Hairline Borders:** `#E5E7EB` for light surfaces; `#1F2430` for midnight structural components.
- **Warning Indicator:** `#F59E0B` (Amber) for statistical drift, low association sample size, and inventory friction.
- **Critical / Danger:** `#EF4444` (Crimson) for rule regression, low margin warnings, and system termination.
- **Secondary Text / Metadata:** `#6B7280` for secondary copy; `#9CA3AF` for tertiary annotations.

## Typography

The type system blends the computational geometry of Geist with the high-density legibility of Inter.

- **Primary Display & Headings (Geist):** Used for analytical headings, large performance counters, and micro-labels. Tight letter-spacing gives tabular and metric visualizations an intentional, engineered rhythm.
- **Body & Data Tables (Inter):** Deployed across table cells, analytical notes, configuration views, and body documentation to maintain neutral legibility across long shifts.
- **Numerical Alignment:** Tabular figures (`tnum`) and slashed zeros must be enabled globally for all monetary values, percentages, Apriori metric outputs (Lift, Support, Confidence), and timestamp strings.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around a persistent, collapsed/expandable midnight navigation sidebar (260px expanded, 72px iconified) paired with a responsive 12-column analytical grid canvas.

### Breakpoints & Adaptability
- **Desktop (>= 1280px):** 12-column grid with 24px (`space-xl`) gutters and 32px (`margin-desktop`) outer padding. High-density data matrices, dual-split recommendation visualizers, and right-rail inspection drawers are native.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8-column layout with 20px (`gutter`) gutters. The midnight sidebar collapses to icon-only rail (72px). Metric grids reflow to 2x2 formations.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with 16px margins. The sidebar recedes into a bottom tab bar or top slide drawer. Apriori data tables convert into stacked swipeable analytical cards.

### Spacing Cadence
Component padding maintains an 8px base rhythm with tight 4px interior increments (`space-xs`) for micro-badges, 8px (`space-sm`) for compact inputs and pill controls, and 20px (`space-lg`) for interior card padding.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids heavy drop shadows, favoring sharp planar surfaces layered over tinted grounds with crisp hairline borders. Depth communicates analytical focus rather than physical weight.

- **Surface 0 (Background Canvas):** Tinted soft canvas (`#F7F8FA`).
- **Surface 1 (Standard Metric & Graph Cards):** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) with a 1px solid hairline border (`#E5E7EB`) and a soft ambient shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(17, 19, 24, 0.04), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(17, 19, 24, 0.02)`.
- **Surface 2 (Hover States & Detached Menus):** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) with a 1px solid `#D1D5DB` border and subtle elevation: `0 10px 15px -3px rgba(17, 19, 24, 0.06), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(17, 19, 24, 0.03)`.
- **Surface 3 (Slide-over Inspection Drawers & Command Palettes):** `#FFFFFF` paired with deep midnight overlays (`rgba(17, 19, 24, 0.5)` backdrop blur 4px). Shadow: `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(17, 19, 24, 0.1), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(17, 19, 24, 0.05)`.
- **Structural Midnight Chrome:** The lateral rail sits at `#111318` without shadows, using an inner right-edge hairline divider (`#1F2430`) to delineate platform control from content space.

## Shapes

The interface balances sharp technical precision with modern enterprise ergonomics:
- **Cards, Modals & Metric Tiles:** 12px to 16px corner radius (`rounded-lg` to `rounded-xl`) to soften large canvas areas without compromising tabular density.
- **Buttons, Text Inputs & Dropdown Triggers:** 8px corner radius (`rounded`) ensuring comfortable click and touch targets.
- **Apriori Micro-badges & Quantitative Tags:** Fully rounded pill shapes (`9999px`) or ultra-compact 4px radius geometry to differentiate operational metadata from interactive buttons.
- **Interactive Graphs & Affinity Nodes:** Circular SKU anchors (100% border-radius) wrapped in 2px hairline boundary rings.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid `#625BF6` fill, `#FFFFFF` Geist 13px weight 600 text, 8px radius, height 36px (compact) or 40px (regular). Hover: `#5048E5`. Active: `#4338CA`.
- **Secondary / Analytical:** Solid `#FFFFFF`, 1px border `#E5E7EB`, `#111318` text. Hover: `#F7F8FA` background, `#2563EB` border/text accent.
- **Ghost / Utility:** Transparent fill, `#6B7280` text, 8px radius. Hover: `#F3F4F6` background, `#111318` text.

### Metric Cards & Analytical Tiles
- Built with a `#FFFFFF` base, 1px `#E5E7EB` outline, and 16px radius.
- Header row houses the metric label (`label-sm` in `#6B7280`), optional status indicator, and icon trigger.
- Center features large counter typography (`metric-stat` in `#111318`).
- Bottom row anchors delta trends (`+14.2% Lift`) with emerald green `#10B981` or warning amber `#F59E0B` micro-pills.

### Apriori Micro-Badges
- Specialized numerical indicators for algorithmic outputs:
  - **Support:** Background `#EFF6FF`, text `#1D4ED8` (Blue 700), border `rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.2)`.
  - **Confidence:** Background `#ECFDF5`, text `#047857` (Emerald 700), border `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.2)`.
  - **Lift:** Background `#EEF2FF`, text `#4338CA` (Indigo 700), border `rgba(98, 91, 246, 0.25)`.
- Typography: `label-mono`, uppercase, letter-spacing 0.06em, padding 2px 6px.

### Data Tables
- Strict 44px row heights for dense readability. Table header uses `#F7F8FA` background, 1px bottom border `#E5E7EB`, text `label-sm` uppercase.
- Alternating row selection states: `#FFFFFF` default, `#F9FAFB` subtle hover. Active row selection highlighted with a 2px left border accent in `#625BF6`.

### Inputs & Command Palette
- **Inputs:** Height 36px, `#FFFFFF` surface, 1px `#E5E7EB` border, 8px radius. Focused state applies a 1px `#625BF6` border and a 3px ring of `rgba(98, 91, 246, 0.15)`.
- **Global Command Palette (`⌘K`):** Centered modal overlay at 640px width. Deep charcoal header input, instant-filtered SKU and rule lists, with keyboard shortcut micro-badges (`kbd`) rendered in `#F3F4F6` with a `#D1D5DB` border.

### Drawers & Graph Visualizers
- **Rule Inspector Drawer:** 460px right-docked panel sliding over content with a 1px left hairline border `#E5E7EB`. Contains antecedent-consequent visual maps, inventory sync state, and confidence sliders.
- **Affinity Graph Nodes:** Nodes represented by 32px-48px circles with garment thumbnail images, bounded by an outer stroke colored according to cluster strength. Dynamic SVG bezier curves represent association affinity, with stroke thickness scaled to relative Lift.
